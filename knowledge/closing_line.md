# Closing Line

No external closing-line source is implemented yet.

Current state:
- TotoAI stores TotoBrief bookmaker probabilities and normalized odds where
  available.
- The project can compare BK probabilities to normalized odds via `study-bk`.
- External odds/statistics sources may be added later.

Do not treat current BK probabilities as a verified closing line unless a future
data source and validation step prove that mapping.
