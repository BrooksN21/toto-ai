## Risks / unresolved design conflicts

- Existing preparation can persist an `unresolved` diagnostic but `prepare_drawing()` itself is not a terminal scheduler result; resource failures currently bubble through CLI. Decide whether emergency FAIL is a new runner decision/marker or a scheduler execution error, and preserve existing NO BET semantics for ordinary modeled EV rejection.
- Freshness is split across TotoBrief raw detail cache metadata, page-one summary, provider schedule timestamps, preparation readiness, and final pinned revalidation. A `mapped_count=15` row alone is insufficient; stale/finished/changed target data must invalidate it.
- `Drawing.result`/`score` are mutable operational columns. The planned immutable result snapshot is needed to preserve corrected official results and audit source hashes; do not use an in-place overwrite as the only history.
- `package-audit` warnings currently do not affect PLAY, and its default 0.95/0.20 thresholds are diagnostic rather than approved production gates. If promoted to a gate, freeze the config in the package/audit hash and report the exact reason; do not silently change category/probability semantics.
- The current 4952 run manifest has `coverage.gate_decision=PENDING` due to fewer than 30 drawings/450 events, but orchestration ignores that diagnostic and still emits PLAY. Do not conflate external-odds coverage PENDING with the requested package-concentration gate.
- The 4952 local DB is incomplete for settlement despite a separate authoritative local fixture/report. Tests must distinguish operational DB state from archived settlement evidence and must not claim observed profitability.
- The request says “terminal FAIL” for morning preparation, while current runner model decisions are `PLAY`, `NO BET`, `FAILED`, `IGNORED` and scheduler outcomes are `bet-ready`, `no-bet`, `failed`, `ignored`; introduce no ambiguous alias without updating manifest/marker invariants and existing tests.
