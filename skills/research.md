# Research

Use this workflow for research modules and CLI commands.

Workflow:
- Define the hypothesis and required data.
- Filter cancelled, void, and missing-result events out of standard backtests.
- Use SQLite data collected from TotoBrief unless the task explicitly adds a
  new source.
- Export reproducible CSV and Markdown reports.
- Keep conclusions descriptive unless a backtest supports them.
- Update [../memory-bank/CURRENT_STATE.md](../memory-bank/CURRENT_STATE.md) and
  related files after completion.
